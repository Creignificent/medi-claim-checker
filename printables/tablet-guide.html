Medi-Claim Checker

Simple guide to reviewing Medicare claims

What to look for

Charges for medical equipment you did not order or receive — especially urinary catheters, braces, orthotics, or wound care supplies from companies you do not know.

Monthly checklist

  • Open your Medicare Summary Notice
  • Look at the name of every company that billed you
  • Ask: “Did I order this? Did I receive it?”
  • If the answer is no, write down the details
  • Call 1-800-MEDICARE to report it

What to say when you call

“I am calling to report a charge I did not order or receive. The company is ________. The date is ________. The amount is $________. They billed for ________.”

Protect your Medicare number

  • Never give it to someone who contacts you first
  • Ignore offers for “free” medical equipment
  • Treat it like a credit card
