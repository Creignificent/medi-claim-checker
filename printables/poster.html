Medi-Claim Checker

Check your Medicare claims.
Stop unauthorized bills.
Look for charges you did not order
especially medical equipment such as catheters, braces, or wound care.
  • Review your Medicare Summary Notice every month
  • Ask: “Did I order this? Did I receive it?”
  • If the answer is no, call Medicare and report it
Call 1-800-MEDICARE
