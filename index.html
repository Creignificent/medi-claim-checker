How to Report a Suspicious Claim

  • Have your Medicare card and the statement in front of you.
  • Note the company name, date of service, and amount billed.
  • Call Medicare at 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227).
  • You can also contact your local Senior Medicare Patrol (SMP) — they help for free.
Call 1-800-MEDICARE

Reporting is free and confidential. You will not get in trouble for reporting a bill you did not authorize.

For Families & Caregivers

You can help a loved one review statements without taking over their care.

  • Offer to sit together once a month and look at the Medicare Summary Notice.
  • Look specifically for medical equipment from companies they don’t recognize.
  • Help them call Medicare if something looks wrong — they should be on the line too.
  • Never give their Medicare number to anyone who contacts you first.
